# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales data using Python to identify trends in product, regional and salesperson performance.

The objective was to use data analysis and visualisation to answer key business questions and provide actionable recommendations.

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which regions perform best?
- Which salespeople generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the most units?
- How does revenue change over time?

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- JupyterLab

## Analysis

The project includes:

- Data exploration and summary statistics
- Filtering and grouping data with Pandas
- Revenue analysis by product
- Revenue analysis by region
- Revenue analysis by salesperson
- Units sold analysis
- Revenue trend analysis
- Data visualisation

## Key Insights

- Phones generated the highest total revenue at **£119,200**.
- The North region generated the highest total revenue at **£86,100**.
- Alice was the highest-revenue salesperson, generating **£86,100**.
- Phones sold the most units overall, with **149 units sold**.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritise Phones:** Maintain strong phone inventory levels and consider increasing marketing investment because phones lead both revenue and units sold.

2. **Investigate Regional Performance:** Examine the factors driving the North region's strong performance and determine whether successful approaches can be replicated in other regions.

3. **Learn From Top Performers:** Review Alice's sales approach and consider sharing successful practices with other salespeople.

## Conclusion

The analysis identified clear differences in performance across products, regions and salespeople.

The results suggest that the company could focus on its strongest product category, investigate successful regional strategies and learn from high-performing salespeople to support future growth.
